#3D Printer Quote Service
Do you have a 3D Printer business?
Do your customers asked you for a quotation for a piece and they have to wait until you slice the piece in your Slicer for you to give them a price?
Well if you do, you noticed this is such a waste of time for you and your customers.

This project gives a solution to automatize the process of giving a client a quotation for their piece.
